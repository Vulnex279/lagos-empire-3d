# Continuation checklist

1. Review the delivered screenshots, APK and verification report. The upgraded
   source is launchable and the Android package is build-verified; preserve this
   checkpoint while continuing the art and gameplay work below.
2. Install the resulting APK on the intended 6 GB Samsung phone. Test a full intro
   quest, all three cars, room/duplex/mansion transitions, multitouch, background/
   resume and saves. Profile 20 minutes per preset; 30 FPS remains a target.
3. Art direction still needs a production pass: more natural clothing deformation,
   diverse NPC appearances, richer vehicle surfaces/interiors, cohesive Lagos
   architecture, denser streets, weather and better ground materials. Imported art
   improves the prototype but should not be described as final premium quality.
4. Replace decorative sidewalk motion with obstacle-aware pedestrian navigation;
   add traffic simulation and branching owner dialogue. Current schedules and
   distance activation are lightweight and bounded; no general NPC pathfinding.
5. Expand procedural contract consequences, ownership-visitation requirements for
   all businesses, employee/manager choices, insurance, vehicle restoration and
   fleet operations. Current rivals are a daily sector-pressure simulation, not
   full autonomous acquisition agents or partnership negotiations.
6. Deep late-game content remains: corporate acquisition choices, hotel chains,
   developments, rare vehicle contracts, expanded relationships, difficulty
   presets and Legacy Mode. Seven business categories and safe large balances are
   a working foundation, not completion of every Living Empire directive.
7. Obtain an empty approved GitHub repository or creation permission. Push the
   local history, then run the prepared workflow and verify uploaded artifacts.
   Do not substitute an unrelated repository or publish publicly without approval.
8. Longer balance playtests: the automated fixed portfolio produces roughly
   ₦50 million after 3,000 days, with loss-making periods and no debt in tested
   scenarios. This does not establish enjoyable first-hour or endgame pacing.
9. Multiple save slots, exact world parking transforms, destructive Android storage
   fault injection and real-device corruption recovery remain future work.

Resume with project.godot, docs/UPGRADE_AUDIT.md, tools/validate.sh and
`docs/ANDROID_BUILD.md`. Asset origins and adaptations are in ASSET_LICENSES.md.
