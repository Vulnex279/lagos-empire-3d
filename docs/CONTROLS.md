# Controls

| Action | Android | Desktop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walk | Left virtual joystick | WASD or arrows |
| Run | RUN toggle | Hold Shift |
| Look | Drag right-side world area | Hold right mouse and move |
| Interact / enter / exit | USE | E |
| Phone | PHONE button | Tab |
| Pause | PAUSE / Android Back | Escape |
| Accelerate | Joystick up | W / Up |
| Steer | Joystick left/right | A/D or arrows |
| Brake then reverse | Joystick down | S / Down |
| Hard brake | Hold BRAKE | Space |

Stop before exiting or delivering a passenger. If both doors and the rear are blocked, move into open space before exiting. A gold waypoint indicates the active job, selected map destination or next introductory objective.

Touch movement and camera look track separate finger indices. Releasing the joystick stops movement. Menus clear touch state and pause physics and time.

Service an owned vehicle at Eko Motors. Retrieve or store it at Eko Motors, or the mansion garage after purchase. The company car is supplied and serviced for passenger contracts and cannot be sold. A saved owned vehicle is retrieved at Eko Motors after loading; exact vehicle parking transforms are not persisted in this version.

Use the floor marker near the interior door to exit, rest or furnish a home. Rest advances a whole economic day, including rent, interest, business operations and job deadlines. Menu time itself does not advance the simulation.
